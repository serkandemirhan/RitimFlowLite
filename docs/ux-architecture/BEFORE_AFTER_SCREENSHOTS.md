# Ekran görüntüleri

Önceki fabrika yolculuğu görüntüleri `../factory-journey/screenshots/desktop/factory-20261102-after-operator-fix/` ve `../factory-journey/screenshots/mobile/factory-20261102-ux-verified/` altında korunmuştur. Son kaynak ve paket görüntüleri sırasıyla `../factory-journey/screenshots/desktop/architecture-isolated-20261102-source-03/`, `../factory-journey/screenshots/mobile/architecture-isolated-20261102-source-03/`, `../factory-journey/screenshots/desktop/architecture-isolated-20261102-packaged-02/` ve `../factory-journey/screenshots/mobile/architecture-isolated-20261102-packaged-02/` altındadır.

Son gerçek Keycloak fabrika yolculuğu görüntüleri `../factory-journey/screenshots/desktop/architecture-true-final-source-20261102/`, `../factory-journey/screenshots/mobile/architecture-true-final-source-20261102/`, `../factory-journey/screenshots/desktop/architecture-true-final-package-20261102/` ve `../factory-journey/screenshots/mobile/architecture-true-final-package-20261102/` altındadır. Gerçek cihaz testi yerine Chrome görünüm genişliği emülasyonu kullanılmıştır.
