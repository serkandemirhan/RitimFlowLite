# B03C — existing frontend physical and operation rules

Read-only domain analysis. Frontend files and data are unchanged.

- `quantity-model.js` derives a job balance from its own plan, direct receipt, accepted incoming transfers, outgoing transfers and recorded good/scrap/pending/setup/rework. The order's ordered quantity is a separate measure. Historical production counts do not themselves establish a transferable physical lot.
- `transfer-ledger.js` treats direct received input, accepted same-step transfers and accepted operation inputs as distinct lots; transfer dispatch removes available source quantity immediately. In-transit quantities remain separate until explicit destination acceptance. Production consumption reduces source lot availability; corruption produces issues rather than repair.
- `transfer-service.js` requires manager, distinct jobs of the same order and same internal operation, a valid source lot, reason, open jobs, unprocessed source quantity and destination unstarted capacity including pending transfers and reservations. Acceptance rechecks target capacity and state. Duplicate issue/accept is rejected. Reverse requires physical return and accepted stock must be unused. Tests demonstrate 250 ordered / 150 planned / 100 received, 25 physical remainder vs 50 unstarted plan, no duplicate acceptance and concurrent pending destination capacity.
- `planning-service.js` capacity for the first operation is order quantity plus authorized compensation minus plans. Later internal operations require available closed, approved good output from the preceding operation; plan creation reserves source output. `operation-ledger.js` tracks reserved → sent/in-transit → accepted, and enforces the immediate predecessor, route key and origin job. Fason in the middle of a route is unsupported. No output should be inferred from historical production records without provenance.
- Material receipt and allocation are separate: material reception records order-level quantity and unit conversion; allocation to an existing job is bounded by free received material and the job's plan. Direct job physical receipt is bounded by its allocation. Compensation requires documented loss and does not increase original customer order quantity. Existing P0/P1 tests verify partial receipt/allocation and transaction guards.

Backend must preserve these numerical and lineage rules. Unsupported reverse, production consumption, adjacent-operation approved-good reservation, supplier flows and loss-reconciled compensation remain explicitly closed until authoritative backend commands exist; missing historical provenance is not synthesized.
